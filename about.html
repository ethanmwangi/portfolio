Music

Music has always been a source of inspiration and grounding for me, and my current favorites reflect the range of what I look for in sound. I’m drawn to artists like Little Simz and Dave for their lyrical depth, storytelling, and the way they navigate personal and social themes with intelligence and emotion. Their music challenges and resonates with me in equal measure. On the other end of the spectrum, I find comfort in the timeless soul of Sade, the laid-back, experimental indie tones of Mac DeMarco, and the mellow, atmospheric beats of Nujabes. Each of these artists brings a distinct mood — whether it's calm introspection, emotional honesty, or subtle complexity — that mirrors how I experience and use music in my life and creative work. Ultimately, my taste reflects a balance between energy and stillness, rhythm and reflection — music that makes me think, feel, and create.


My Faith

Above all, my love for God shapes who I am and how I move through the world. My faith gives me perspective, peace, and a sense of purpose in everything I do — creatively and personally. It’s a relationship that grounds me in gratitude and humility, and it influences the way I approach life: with intention, compassion, and a desire to grow. Whether in quiet moments or in the midst of challenges, my connection to God is a constant source of strength and direction.
